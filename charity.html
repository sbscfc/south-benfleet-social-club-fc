Charity is at the heart of South Benfleet Social Club FC. We use football to bring people together while supporting worthwhile causes.

Supporting Our Community

South Benfleet Social Club FC was created with the belief that football can be about more than what happens on the pitch.

Through charity matches, we want to bring players, supporters and the wider community together while raising money and awareness for causes that matter.
