Inaugural Charity Match

South Benfleet Social Club FC
vs
Wickford Town Reserves FC

1 – 3
Sunday 26th July 2026
Len Forge Centre

The Match

South Benfleet Social Club FC played their inaugural match on Sunday 26th July, welcoming Wickford Town Reserves FC to the Len Forge Centre for a special charity fixture in aid of St Luke's Hospice.

It was a historic occasion for SBSCFC, as the club took to the pitch for the very first time.

Hurren Makes History

SBSCFC started brightly and were rewarded with the opening goal.

A ball was played through towards Dan Hurren, who found himself in behind the Wickford defence. With the goalkeeper advancing, Hurren calmly — or perhaps neatly, as we'll put it — slotted the ball beyond the oncoming keeper to send SBSCFC into a 1–0 lead.

SBSCFC then thought they had doubled their advantage later in the first half.

A defensive mix-up at the back from Wickford Town Reserves allowed the ball to fall kindly to Callum Webb, who reacted quickest and toe-poked the ball into the bottom corner.

Unfortunately for Webb and SBSCFC, the flag was raised for offside and the goal was ruled out.

Wickford began to pile on the pressure as the half progressed, but despite creating pressure and looking increasingly dangerous, they couldn't find the equaliser before the break.

Half-time: South Benfleet Social Club FC 1–0 Wickford Town Reserves FC

Wickford Turn It Around

The second half saw Wickford Town Reserves come out with renewed purpose, putting SBSCFC under increasing pressure.

Terry Griffin was called upon on numerous occasions and produced a number of fantastic saves to keep SBSCFC in front.

However, the Wickford pressure eventually told.

With around 20 minutes remaining, Wickford were awarded a penalty after an SBSCFC full-back was adjudged to have handled the ball inside the area.

The Wickford penalty taker made no mistake, calmly converting from the spot to bring the scores level at 1–1.

From that point onwards, it became very much one-way traffic.

Wickford Town Reserves added two further goals to complete the turnaround and ultimately came away with a 3–1 victory.

Full-time: South Benfleet Social Club FC 1–3 Wickford Town Reserves FC

Terry Griffin

A fantastic performance between the sticks, with Griffin producing a number of excellent saves to keep SBSCFC in the game during the second half.
