The Story Of SBSCFC

OUR STORY

From our first charity match in 2026 to our ambition of building a side ready for competitive football.

More Than Just A Football Club

South Benfleet Social Club FC was formed in 2026 with a simple idea: bring people together through football, friendship, community and charity.

The club is currently focused on charity football matches, giving players and supporters the chance to enjoy the game while helping raise money for worthwhile causes.

While the club is only at the beginning of its journey, we want to build something that can grow with the local community.

Our Journey

2026

The Beginning

South Benfleet Social Club FC was formed, creating the foundations for a new community football club.

26 July 2026

Our First Match

SBSCFC played its inaugural match against Wickford Town Reserves FC in a charity fixture at the Len Forge Centre.

2026/27

Building The Club

Our focus is on playing charity matches, growing the squad, building our identity and becoming part of the local football community.

The Future

The Road To 2027/28

Our ambition is to build towards having a side ready to compete for the 2027/28 season.

The Road To 2027/28

BUILD. GROW. COMPETE.

Charity football is where our journey begins. The long-term ambition is to build a strong squad, establish the club within the local football community and be ready to take the next step into competitive football.

We know there is plenty of work to do between now and then, but we're excited about what the future can bring.

Football With A Purpose

Charity will remain an important part of what South Benfleet Social Club FC stands for.

Our inaugural fixture against Wickford Town Reserves FC was played in aid of St Luke's Hospice, and we hope to continue using football to support worthwhile causes as the club grows.

This Is Only The Beginning

FOOTBALL. COMMUNITY. CHARITY.

From the players on the pitch to the people supporting the club off it, everyone is part of the journey.

South Benfleet Social Club FC was formed in 2026. What comes next is up to all of us.
